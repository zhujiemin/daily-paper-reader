## 摘要
随着生成式AI在动漫风格图像创作中的应用日益广泛，区分人工绘制、AI局部重绘和文本到图像生成的图像，对于版权归属、视觉溯源和内容治理具有重要意义。现有的AI生成图像检测器主要面向真实世界照片，往往忽略动漫特有的线索，如平涂上色、夸张结构和艺术性线条控制。为弥补这一空白，我们提出AniPrO，一个面向可解释动漫图像溯源的多维描述增强框架。AniPrO基于AnimeDL-2M构建，包含来自35,000张图像候选池的15,000个平衡样本，涵盖Real、Inpainting和Text2Image类别，并配有结构化的五维描述。我们进一步提出AniPrO-SFD-Bench和AniPrO-MFR-Bench，从统计特征判别和多模态融合推理两个角度评估溯源检测。实验表明，结构化语义引导能够揭示系统性的AI生成偏差，例如全局视觉合理性与局部细节连贯性之间的差距，并提升对具有挑战性的局部重绘样本的检测性能。数据集和代码将发布于：https://github.com/YAN-LIU05/AniPrO。

## Abstract
As generative AI becomes increasingly used in anime-style image creation, distinguishing human-drawn, AI-inpainted, and text-to-image images is important for copyright attribution, visual provenance, and content governance. Existing AI-generated image detectors mainly target real-world photographs and often overlook anime-specific cues such as flat coloring, exaggerated structures, and artistic line control. To address this gap, we propose AniPrO, a multi-dimensional description-enhanced framework for interpretable anime image provenance. Built upon AnimeDL-2M, AniPrO contains 15,000 balanced samples from a 35,000-image candidate pool, covering Real, Inpainting, and Text2Image categories with structured five-dimensional descriptions. We further introduce AniPrO-SFD-Bench and AniPrO-MFR-Bench to evaluate provenance detection from statistical feature discrimination and multimodal fusion reasoning perspectives. Experiments show that structured semantic guidance reveals systematic AI-generation biases, such as the gap between global visual plausibility and local detail coherence, and improves the detection of challenging inpainting samples. The dataset and code will be released at: https://github.com/YAN-LIU05/AniPrO.
